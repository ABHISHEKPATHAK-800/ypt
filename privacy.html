DuoTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

DuoTrack is a study timer and progress tracker for study partners. This page explains what information the app uses.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and login ID. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google name, email address and a unique Google account ID. We do not access anything else in your Google account.
  • Study data: timer sessions, subjects, to-dos, mock test scores, questions solved and similar progress you enter.
  • Content you add: chat messages, profile pictures, and files or images you upload inside the app.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to log you in, show your timer and progress, and sync it with your study partner. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase. Some settings and a login session are also kept in your browser on your own device.

Sharing

Your study data and chat are visible to your study partner and to the app administrator. We do not share your information with anyone else, except where required by law.

Your choices
